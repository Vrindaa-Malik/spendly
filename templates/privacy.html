Privacy Policy

How Spendly collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to run Spendly:

  • Account details, such as your name and email address.
  • Expense records you enter, including amount, category, date, and description.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type, needed to keep the service working.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide and improve Spendly: to sign you in, store and display your expenses, and generate your spending summaries. We do not use your financial data for advertising.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial information. We may disclose information only when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of Spendly and its users.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including storing passwords in hashed form. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Spendly does not connect to your bank accounts or store payment card details.

5. Your Rights

You can view, edit, or delete your expenses at any time. You may also delete your account, which removes your personal information and expense records from Spendly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Spendly after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
